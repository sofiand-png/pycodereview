🚨 Potential New Rule Candidates
1. Architecture Guide

Source: 

crss_architecture_guide

A. “Process, Not Thread Isolation”

Document says:

“Prefer process isolation over thread isolation”

“Threads increase unpredictability and risk”

➡ Potential Rule:
“Threading in non-critical code must comply with bounded, deterministic behaviors; threads MUST NOT be used in Strict-A regardless of phase.”

(This may duplicate or extend existing rules.)

B. “Critical Code Must Be Minimal”

States critical logic must be:

Small

Deterministic

Side-effect free

➡ Potential Rule:
Hard-limit maximum cyclomatic complexity or max number of branches inside @critical.

C. “Stateless-by-default”

Says state must be explicit and controlled.

➡ Potential Rule:
Forbid implicit global state usage across Strict-A code.

2. Phase-Aware Interpretation Model

Source: 

crss_phase_aware_rule_interpret…

D. “Interaction with external systems of any kind is forbidden in critical code”

This is currently implied but not explicitly codified as a MUST rule.

➡ Could be formalized under I/O / determinism rules.

E. “Operations that may trigger GC”

Interpretation model forbids this, but Core/Strict rules don’t explicitly mention GC suppression in phase-aware rules.

➡ A new rule may be needed.

F. “Results must be frozen or immutable before entering critical execution”

Important constraint, not clearly codified as a rule.

➡ Should probably be a Strict rule.
